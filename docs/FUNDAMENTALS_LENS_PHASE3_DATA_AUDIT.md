## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

In accordance with the **Phase 3 Evidence Assurance** mandate for *Fundamentals Lens*, this audit conducted an exhaustive, line-item reconciliation of all 375 reported financial numbers in `datasets/fundamentals_lens/financial_facts_5yr.json` against primary regulatory filings (SEC Form 10-K / 10-K/A for US issuers, BSE/NSE Annual Reports and Audited Financial Results for Indian issuers).

The audit verified:
1. **Source Authenticity:** Every observation maps to an authentic, primary regulatory document with accession number or BSE filing identifier.
2. **Line-Item Lineage:** Primary financial statements (e.g. *Consolidated Statements of Operations*, *Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows*) and specific line items (e.g. *"Payments for property and equipment"*, *"Revenue from operations"*) are recorded for every single observation.
3. **Zero Synthetic Coercion:** Missing data is never coerced to zero. Where a company incurs a net loss or near-zero profit, derived ratios (such as Cash Conversion) are explicitly qualified and suppressed rather than allowed to produce economically misleading distortions.
4. **Consistency of Scope:** 100% of reported figures reflect the **consolidated** entity under audited US GAAP or Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS).

## 3. Primary Statement Line-Item Mappings

Every metric is reconciled to standardized primary disclosure line items across accounting regimes:

| Metric Code | US GAAP Primary Statement & Line Items | Indian Ind AS Primary Statement & Line Items |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Revenue** | *Consolidated Statements of Operations / Income*<br>• `Total net sales`<br>• `Total revenues` | *Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss*<br>• `Revenue from operations` |
| **Operating Income** | *Consolidated Statements of Operations / Income*<br>• `Operating income`<br>• `Income from operations` | *Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss*<br>• `Profit before exceptional items and tax` (operating proxy)<br>• `Operating profit` |
| **Net Income** | *Consolidated Statements of Operations / Income*<br>• `Net income`<br>• `Net income attributable to shareholders` | *Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss*<br>• `Profit for the year / period`<br>• `Profit attributable to owners of the parent` |
| **Operating Cash Flow** | *Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows*<br>• `Cash generated by / provided by operating activities`<br>• `Net cash provided by operating activities` | *Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows*<br>• `Net cash generated from operating activities`<br>• `Cash flow from operating activities` |
| **Capital Expenditure** | *Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows*<br>• `Payments for property and equipment`<br>• `Additions to property, plant and equipment` | *Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows*<br>• `Purchase of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets`<br>• `Capital expenditure` |
